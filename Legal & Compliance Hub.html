Protecting your idea, website & strategy UK — PLAIN-ENGLISH GUIDE

You can’t copyright an idea itself — but you can protect how you express and run it. Here’s what actually applies, and the related items are tracked in the list below.

Copyright — automatic

Your website, content, designs, code & strategy docs are protected the moment they’re recorded. Keep dated copies & mark them ©.

Trademark — register it

The name & logo “Common Plate” aren’t automatic. Register with the UK IPO (from ~£170) to own the brand.

Assign IP to the company

Make sure founders & contractors sign IP-assignment terms, so the company — not an individual — owns everything.

Keep the strategy confidential

Use NDAs before sharing the plan. Your aggregated dataset may also carry UK database rights.

Ltd → charity: decide the route

A charity, a CIO, or a CIC are different structures with different rules. Pick one before registering — it shapes governance & tax.

Charity Commission

Register once income & structure qualify. You’ll need charitable objects, trustees, and a governing document.

Tax follows structure

Charitable status changes Corporation Tax, VAT & Gift Aid treatment — worth modelling before you switch.
